🏆 The SYW Monthly Winner

The overall winner is determined primarily by daily participation points accumulated through the month. The winner gets the platform to be primary speaker/feature for the next General Session and subsequent events — because SYW doesn't just clap for people, it hands the mic to whoever showed up the most. The moderator can be appointed separately.

🎯 The SYW Monthly Challenge

Every month, members vote on one challenge. It should be specific + measurable + slightly uncomfortable + fun. The point isn't to overwhelm — it's to collectively do something outside our normal routine.

  • Publish for 7 consecutive days
  • Pitch three potential clients
  • Learn one new skill and teach it
  • Apply for five opportunities
  • Write 500 words for five days
  • Record and publish your first video
  • Reach out to five people
  • Complete a project you've been postponing
  • Read 20 pages daily for a week
